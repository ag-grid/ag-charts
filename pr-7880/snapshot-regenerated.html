01How to read the ranking

Pattern / gradient fills re-tile. A ≤1px shift of a hatch- or gradient-filled shape re-lands every fill pixel, so the whole shape reads as changed. High pixel counts there mean a small move, not a big one.
Small-canvas tests inflate the percentage. Axis-strip / isolated-component snapshots are small, so a 1px shift is a large fraction. Trust the absolute pixel count.
